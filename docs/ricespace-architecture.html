RiceSpace / system map

A site on a network with no centre.

The hosted Rails site, shared signed-feed core, CLI peers, and the paths that move verified feeds. The site is one node; direct sync does not depend on it.

Trust boundary

Device-key-pinned TLS carries sync end to end. Hash-chained feed records are signature-verified before storage or rendering. A relay forwards ciphertext and can still observe timing and size, or drop traffic.

Discovery is not trust

Mainline DHT results are address hints. Endpoint slots and per-follow tickets are signed; pair-scoped ticket slots are public, not encrypted. Relay candidates still have to complete the protocol, and the peer's device pin remains authoritative.

Availability

RiceSpace operates no relay fleet. The checked-in volunteer-relay list is empty. When direct paths fail, a reachable open volunteer relay is required for live sync.

Derived from the current Rails routes, shared Ruby P2P implementation, CLI entry points, and internet-networking specification.
